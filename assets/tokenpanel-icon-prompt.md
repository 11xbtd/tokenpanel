## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one production-ready app icon for TokenPanel, a refined AI token usage and quota dashboard. A single bold custom geometric T monogram formed by two interlocking rounded token/panel tiles. Ivory horizontal top tile and an ivory vertical stem, with one restrained mint offset edge suggesting a second panel and measured token flow. Deep emerald / petrol-green rounded-square background, subtle tonal gradient and very soft dimensional edge lighting. Premium modern developer-tool identity, calm, precise, distinctive, beautifully balanced. Clean mostly flat vector-like geometry with excellent optical weight and strong silhouette at 16px and 32px. Front view, perfectly centered, square 1024x1024 composition, the rounded-square icon fills the canvas with only transparent corners. Generous but compact inner margins, thick simple forms, no thin lines or tiny details. The only letter is the abstract T mark; no wordmark, no additional text, no graphs, no rising arrow, no coin pile, no dollar signs, no sparkles, no circuit-board clutter, no mockup, no border outside the icon, no cast shadow beyond the rounded square, no watermark. Real transparent alpha outside the rounded-square corners.
